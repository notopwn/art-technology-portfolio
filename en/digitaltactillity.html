TOUCH. READ. SEE AGAIN.

DigitalTactillity

From making,
to seeing anew.

Make a gesture. Read the AI's critique.
Return to your own movement with fresh eyes.
The whole encounter is the artwork.

Letters to read become material to touch.

THE ARTWORK

It begins at your fingertips.
It continues when you look again.

I recorded hand movements and organized their trajectories and rhythms so an AI could read them. When the AI responds with a written critique, the user sees their own gestures from another perspective. Both making the gestures and interpreting that critique belong to the artwork.

01 / ARTIST

With no particular goal,
I move my hand.

Lines, pauses, and repetitions leave traces and rhythms. The user first becomes the artist, making the movement.

FROM GESTURE TO LANGUAGE

A passing gesture,
a trace you can read.

Movement on a screen can disappear into the sensation of the moment. I recorded where and when trajectories, changes in speed, repetitions, and pauses occurred.

Two gestures can share an average speed yet unfold very differently. Rather than reduce them to a few numbers, I kept their shapes and sequence.

The screens on the right show actual recordings from sessions separate from the visual experience shown here.

A DIFFERENT KIND OF AI

AI does not draw for me.
It reads what I have made.

I did not ask AI to guess the person's inner state. Its critique is limited to forms observable in the recording. It must not assume the maker's emotions or intentions; what the critique means remains for the user to decide.

ACTUAL CRITIQUE / EXCERPT

“Direction here is not a destination;
it is something repeatedly renegotiated.”

Direction is not a destination,
but something repeatedly renegotiated.

Paraphrased from the actual output, “A Choreography of Provisional Contact.”

The AI's words offer another perspective on turns and repetitions I may not have noticed. In accepting that critique or reading it differently, the user becomes a viewer again.

ARTIST + VIEWER

My own gesture returns
as an unfamiliar artwork.

The user is both artist and viewer.
The gesture, the AI's critique, and the person reading it again.
Together, they form the artwork.
