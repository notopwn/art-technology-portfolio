ABOUT THE WORK

From building screens
to shaping experiences.

Through these two projects, I kept noticing how an interface could make me read my own actions and materials differently. My gestures returned to me through AI critique, while the order in which I chose PDFs became a record I could use again.

I developed both apps with coding agents. I defined what to make and refined how touch was recorded, the limits of critique, the folder views, and the PDF assembly workflow.
